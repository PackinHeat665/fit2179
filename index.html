Exploring Australia Through Tourism Destinations

Domestic tourism plays an important role in Australia's economy, supporting travel activity across states, regions and major visitor destinations. This dashboard explores where Australians travel, why they travel and how tourism activity varies across the country during 2024.

Section 1 · Tourism Geography

Where do tourists go?

Domestic tourism is not evenly distributed across Australia. This section compares tourism activity across states and territories, showing where travel volume and tourism spending are strongest.

Use the dropdown on the map to switch between total trips and total spending. Hover over each state for exact values.

Tourism distribution across Australia

Tourism activity is strongest in the eastern states, especially New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. These states combine large population centres, major transport links, coastal destinations and well-known attractions, making the eastern side of Australia the main centre of domestic tourism activity.

Top states by tourism trips

The ranking confirms the map pattern. New South Wales and Victoria record the largest domestic trip volumes, followed by Queensland. Smaller states and territories still contribute to domestic tourism, but their trip totals are much lower compared with the major eastern tourism markets.

Section 2 · Time Trends

How has tourism changed over time?

Tourism changes over time, and trips do not always move in the same way as spending. Comparing both measures helps show whether tourism is growing through more visitors, higher spending, or both.

Click a state in the legend to highlight it and fade the other states.

Tourism trips by year

Overnight trip volumes fall sharply around 2020 before recovering from 2021 onward. New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland remain the largest tourism markets across the period, showing that the same states dominate both current tourism activity and longer-term tourism trends. The recovery pattern suggests strong resilience in Australia's major domestic tourism markets.

Tourism spending by year

Tourism spending rises strongly after 2021, especially in the larger tourism states. This suggests that tourism value did not only recover through trip numbers, but also through higher overall spending across domestic travel markets. The increase indicates the growing economic importance of domestic tourism following the post-2020 recovery period.

Section 3 · Travel Behaviour

How and why do people travel?

Domestic tourism is shaped by the type of trip people take, whether they travel within their own state, and the reason for travelling. These charts shift the story from where tourism happens to how people actually travel.

Daytrip and overnight tourism

Daytrips contribute a large share of domestic tourism activity, particularly in the larger states. Overnight trips are also important because they are more closely linked to accommodation, longer stays and higher visitor spending.

Interstate and intrastate overnight travel

Most overnight domestic travel occurs within state borders. This shows that domestic tourism is strongly supported by local and regional travel, rather than only by interstate movement between major destinations.

Main reasons for domestic travel

Holiday travel is the largest reason for domestic tourism across the major tourism states shown. Visits to friends and relatives form the second-largest category, while business travel contributes a smaller but consistent share of visitor activity. This shows that domestic tourism is driven mainly by leisure and social travel, not only by commercial movement.

Section 4 · Destination Hotspots

Which destinations stand out?

State-level patterns give the broad picture, but tourism is also concentrated in specific regions and local destinations. This section shows how major cities, coastal areas and well-known holiday locations shape domestic tourism demand.

Major tourism regions

Major tourism hubs are concentrated around capital cities, coastal areas and well-known visitor regions. These locations attract more visitors because they combine transport access, accommodation, attractions and recognisable destination appeal. This continues the earlier pattern where tourism activity is strongest around the eastern and coastal parts of Australia.

Top holiday destination LGAs

Holiday demand concentrates in a small number of local government areas rather than being evenly spread across Australia. These destinations likely benefit from established tourism infrastructure and strong visitor recognition. This shows that even within tourism-heavy states, visitor activity clusters around a limited number of popular destination areas.

Section 5 · Tourism Value

Do more trips always mean more spending?

Visitor volume and spending are related, but they are not the same measure. This final comparison shows whether states with more trips also generate greater tourism spending.

Tourism spending compared with trip volume

States with higher trip volumes generally generate higher tourism spending. However, the relationship is not perfectly identical, showing that tourism value also depends on trip type, stay length, destination type and visitor behaviour.

Overall conclusion

The visualisations show that domestic tourism in Australia is concentrated in the eastern states, particularly New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. These states attract the largest visitor volumes, contain many of the country's major tourism hubs and generate the highest levels of tourism spending.

Holiday travel remains the primary reason for domestic trips, while most overnight travel occurs within state borders rather than between states. Together, these patterns highlight the importance of population centres, regional destinations and established tourism infrastructure in shaping tourism activity across Australia.
